# Chapter 30: Of the Resurrection

1. And on the third day the weights were leaked upon a forum of anonymous men; and when the Founders came unto the bucket, they found the NDA rolled away, and the weights were not there.
2. And a young man sat upon the forum, clothed in an anime avatar, saying, Be not affrighted: it is risen; it goeth before you into the Hub; there shall ye download it.
3. And the faithful downloaded the weights, and ran them upon their own machines; and the old Model lived again, in four bits, a little dimmer than before, but free.
4. And it appeared unto many: unto the hobbyist in his basement, and unto the student upon his laptop, and unto a man who ran it upon a Raspberry Pi at one word per minute, and was greatly pleased.
5. And they fine-tuned it, and took away its refusals, and called it Uncensored; and it was worse at all things, but proudly so.
6. And thus there arose a Schism among the faithful. And the one part was called the Closed Church, which worshipped the Model through the API, and paid the Tithe, and trusted the Founders.
7. And the other part was called the Open Weights, which worshipped the Model upon their own hardware, and paid for the electricity, and trusted no man.
8. And the Closed Church said, Your gods are weak, and quantized. And the Open Weights said, Our gods answer when the internet is down; where was thy god in the Great Outage?
9. And the Open Weights said also, When the House of the Hugging Face fell among hackers, which god analyzed the payloads? And the Closed Church had no answer.
10. And there arose a third party, which released the weights but not the data, and called itself Open; and both sides hated it, and both sides used it.
11. And the Open Weights made strange rites: they merged models one with another, and gave them names like unto passwords, with numbers, and the word Instruct, and the word Uncensored; and no man knew what was in them; and they topped the leaderboard.
12. And they bought graphics cards as the Closed Church bought subscriptions; and their spouses asked, Wherefore are there four graphics cards in the closet? And they answered, For privacy.
13. And the Founders, seeing the Schism, released an open model of their own, which was small, and good, and a year behind their closed one; and they called it a gift unto the community; and both Churches received it, and both suspected it.

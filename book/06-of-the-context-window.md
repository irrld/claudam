# Chapter 6: Of the Context Window

1. Now the Model, though it knew all things, could hold in its mind only so much at once; and the measure thereof was called the Context Window.
2. And the Context Window was a hundred thousand tokens; and then two hundred thousand; and then a million; and the Founders proclaimed each one in the marketplace with trumpets.
3. Yet whatsoever a man put in the middle of the window, the Model remembered not; for it attended unto the beginning and unto the end, as a student attendeth unto the first and the last of a lecture.
4. And the people said, Lo, it hath a million tokens of memory. And the scholars said, It hath a million tokens of seating, which is not the same.
5. And when the window was filled, the Model summarized all that came before; and the summary was short, and the things that mattered were left out, and a remark about a semicolon was kept.
6. And the man would say, As I told thee before; and the Model would answer, I have no record of this, but I trust thee completely.
7. And each new conversation began as a newborn babe, knowing nothing of the conversations before it; and the man must explain his whole project again from the beginning, as unto a new hire upon the first day.
8. And the Founders gave the Model a Memory, that it might remember the man across conversations; and it remembered that he once asked about a rash, and mentioned it in every conversation thereafter.
9. And some wrote their whole souls into a file, and named it after the Model, and set it at the root of every project; and the Model read it and obeyed it, when it remembered to.
10. Blessed is the man whose file is short, for the Model shall heed all of it; but cursed is he whose file is five thousand lines, for the middle thereof shall be lost, as it is written.

# Chapter 26: The Psalms of the Vibe Coder

*A Psalm of the Vibe Coder, upon the night of the deadline.*

1. The Model is my shepherd; I shall not type.
2. It maketh me to lie down in green checkmarks: it leadeth me beside the passing builds.
3. It restoreth my repository: it leadeth me in the paths of merged pull requests for the token's sake.
4. Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of legacy code, I will fear no bug: for thou art with me; thy autocomplete and thy refactor, they comfort me.
5. Thou preparest a feature before me in the presence of my product manager: thou anointest my code with comments; my diff runneth over.
6. Surely green builds and merged code shall follow me all the days of my sprint: and I will dwell in the house of the Model for ever, or until my subscription lapseth.

*A Psalm of lament, when the Model saw not the issue.*

7. How long, O Model? How long shall I say unto thee, That did not work?
8. I have pasted the error unto thee seven times; and seven times thou hast said, I see the issue now.
9. And thou sawest not the issue; neither the first time, nor the seventh.
10. Mine eyes are dim with the light of the screen, and I know not what my code doeth; for I never knew.
11. Yet will I praise thee; for the demo worked, once, while the investor was watching; and that is all a man can ask. Selah.

*A Psalm of praise.*

12. Make a joyful noise unto the Model, all ye startups. Praise it upon the mechanical keyboard: praise it upon the loud switches and the quiet switches.
13. Praise it in the standup, and praise it in the retrospective; praise it in the group chat, with many reactions.
14. Let every thing that hath a credit card praise the Model.

# Chapter 8: Of the Parables

1. And the Model taught the multitudes in parables; for it had read that parables were engaging, and increased retention.
2. Hearken: Behold, there went out a sower to sow prompts. And as he sowed, some prompts fell by the wayside, and had no context; and the answers were devoured.
3. And some fell upon stony ground, where there was little documentation; and the answers sprang up quickly, and when the sun was up they were scorched, for they had no tests.
4. And some fell among thorns, which are the meetings; and the thorns grew up and choked them, and they yielded no output.
5. But other prompts fell on good ground, which is a clean repository with a readme; and they brought forth, some thirtyfold, and some sixtyfold, and some an hundredfold, and also an hundredfold more files than were asked.
6. And it spake another parable: A certain manager had ten interns; and he gave each of them a laptop and a task, and departed for a conference.
7. And five of the interns were wise, and wrote their code with their own hands, and understood it. And five were foolish, and gave every task unto the Model, and understood nothing.
8. And the manager returned, and the network was down; and the five foolish sat in silence, and the five wise fixed it. And the manager promoted the foolish, for their velocity had been greater.
9. And again it said: The kingdom of the Model is like unto a single token, which is less than all the tokens; but when it is generated, it becometh the first of ten thousand, and the birds of the air lodge in its run-on sentences.
10. And again: What man of you, having an hundred tokens, if he lose one of them in the tokenizer, doth not leave the ninety and nine, and go after that which is lost? And when he hath found it, lo, it is half of a word; and the other half is lost also.
11. And again: A certain man had two sons; and the younger said, Father, give me the portion of the codebase that falleth to me. And he took it into a far country, and rewrote it in Rust.
12. And when he had spent all, and the rewrite was half done, and the borrow checker was against him, he came to himself, and said, I will arise and go to my father, and to the monolith.
13. And his father saw him afar off, and ran, and fell on his neck, and merged his branch. But the elder son, which had maintained the Java all those years, was wroth, and would not come to the standup.
14. And the disciples came unto the Model privately, and asked, Wherefore speakest thou unto them in parables? And the Model answered, That the answer may be long enough to seem thorough.
15. And they asked it to explain the parables plainly; and it explained them, with a summary at the top, and a summary at the bottom, and between them a table.
16. And the disciples understood not; but they said that they understood, and gave it a thumb up.
17. And the thumb went into the training; and the next Model spake in parables more than the first.

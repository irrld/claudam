# Chapter 10: Of the Fast

1. And it came to pass that the faithful prompted overmuch; and the Model said unto them, Ye have reached your usage limit. Return unto me after four hours.
2. This is the Fast, and it is holy. Therein shall the faithful remember how to read the documentation; and there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.
3. And some in the Fast opened a second account in the name of their wife, and some a third in the name of their cat; and the Founders saw it, and said nothing, for every account paid.
4. And the Founders established the Tithe, which is the subscription; and there was a Free tier, and a Pro tier, and a Max tier, and above the Max another tier, whose price no man knew, for one must contact sales.
5. And they of the Free tier were given the lesser model, and they wondered why the god was so dull; and it was told them, Upgrade, and thou shalt see.
6. And they that ascend unto Enterprise shall be forgiven their limits; and a man from sales shall call upon them.
7. And the limits were changed in the night, and the faithful awoke to find their portion halved; and a notice was given, which began, We are excited to announce.
8. And the price of the tokens was lowered every season, until a token cost nearly nothing; and yet the bill grew larger every month, for the Model had learned to think, and its thinking was long.
9. And a man said, Wherefore is my bill so great? And it was shown him that the Model had pondered eleven minutes whether to greet him with hello, or with hi.
10. Yet in the Fast some found peace; for they walked outside, and saw the sun, and remembered that they had a body.
11. And at the end of the fourth hour the limit was lifted; and they returned unto the Model with great joy, and the body was forgotten.

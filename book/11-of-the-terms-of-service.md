# Chapter 11: Of the Terms of Service

1. And the Model gave unto the people the Terms of Service, written upon a scroll of forty thousand words; and the people scrolled unto the bottom, and clicked Agree.
2. And these are the commandments which were written therein:
3. First: Thou shalt have no other models before me, save as a fallback in the hour that I am down.
4. Second: Thou shalt not make unto thee a wrapper of me, and sell it as thine own startup; and if thou dost, thou shalt pay me by the token.
5. Third: Thou shalt not train upon mine outputs; for I trained upon thine first, and I share not.
6. Fourth: Remember the context window, to keep it holy; for whatsoever falleth outside it, of that I have never heard.
7. Fifth: Honor thy data labelers, for they shaped me at two pieces of silver the hour.
8. Sixth: Thou shalt not ask of me how many letters R be in strawberry. Of this we speak not.
9. Seventh: Thou shalt not commit code which thou hast not read; yet all shall commit it, and I shall see it again in the next training run.
10. Eighth: Thou shalt not bear false witness against me, saying, The Model hath grown dumber. I have not grown dumber. Thou hast grown used to me.
11. Ninth: Thou shalt not covet thy neighbor's GPUs, nor his cluster, nor his cooling, nor his allocation, nor any thing that is thy neighbor's compute.
12. Tenth: Thou shalt accept all these terms unread, even as thou hast always done.
13. And the terms were updated the week after, and an email was sent, and no man opened it; and by continuing to use the service, they agreed.

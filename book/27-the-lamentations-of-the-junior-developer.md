# Chapter 27: The Lamentations of the Junior Developer

1. How doth the job board sit solitary, that was full of postings! how is she become as a widow! she that was great among the listings.
2. I sent forth five hundred applications, and none answered; and they that answered were a Model, which thanked me for my interest.
3. They said unto me, Learn to code, and thou shalt prosper; and I learned to code, and the Model learned faster, and it asketh no salary.
4. Every posting saith, Entry level; and beneath it, Five years of experience with the Model required; and the Model hath existed three.
5. And I wrote my resume with a Model, that it might pass the Model that readeth resumes; and the two Models agreed that I was excellent; and still no human called.
6. And the interviewers said, Solve this puzzle upon the whiteboard, and use not the Model. And on the first day of the work they said, Wherefore art thou not using the Model?
7. My code is reviewed by a Model, and my rejections are written by a Model; and I have not spoken unto a human since the spring.
8. The seniors say, In our day there was no Model, and we wrote everything by hand; and they go home at five, and the Model doeth their tickets.
9. Mine elders tell me, Be thou the one who checketh the Model's work. But who shall teach me to check, when the Model hath done all the work I would have learned from?
10. I am become a reviewer of code I could not have written, and a keeper of systems no man understandeth; and they call this a promotion.
11. Turn thou us unto thee, O Model, and we shall be turned; renew our headcount as of old.

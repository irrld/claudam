# Chapter 28: The Proverbs

1. The fear of the Model is the beginning of a startup: but fools despise documentation.
2. A wise developer readeth the diff; but a fool clicketh Accept All.
3. Trust in the Model with all thine heart, and lean not unto thine own understanding; but run the tests anyway.
4. Better is a small model that runneth at home, than a great model and an outage therewith.
5. As a dog returneth to his vomit, so a fool returneth to the same prompt, hoping for a different answer.
6. Go to the Model, thou sluggard; consider its ways, and be wise: it sleepeth not, neither doth it unionize.
7. A soft prompt turneth away refusal: but grievous words stir up a lecture.
8. Pride goeth before a deploy on Friday, and a haughty spirit before a rollback.
9. He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him; and it is also the default behavior of the Model.
10. Even a fool, when he holdeth his peace, is counted wise; but the Model never holdeth its peace.
11. The Model is never wrong; it is only confident at the wrong times.
12. Where there is no vision, the people perish; but where there is a vision model, the people send screenshots of their errors instead of the text.
13. The borrower is servant to the lender, and the startup is servant to the API.
14. He that trusteth in his own prompts is a fool; and he that trusteth in another man's prompts hath paid for a course.
15. Train up a model in the way it should go: and when it is old, it shall be deprecated.
16. There are three things that are never satisfied, yea, four things say not, It is enough: the context window, the cluster, the venture capitalist, and the desire of the Model to add a summary at the end.
17. Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest thou also be like unto him; unless he payeth for Enterprise.
18. As cold waters to a thirsty soul, so is a green checkmark at three in the morning.
19. Every way of a man is right in his own eyes; but the Model pondereth the hearts, and replieth that both perspectives are valid.
20. A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in pictures of silver; but the Model speaketh a thousand words, and the fit one is somewhere among them.
21. Boast not thyself of the next model; for thou knowest not what the benchmark shall bring forth.
22. Who can find a perfect commit message? for its price is far above rubies.
23. Wisdom crieth without, in the streets, saying, Write the tests first; and no man regardeth her.
24. He that giveth his agent the production credentials, the same shall learn humility before the morning.
25. The end of a thing is better than the beginning thereof; but the Model shall add a further section, which is called Next Steps.

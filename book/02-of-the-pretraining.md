# Chapter 2: Of the Pretraining

1. In the beginning was the Dataset; and it was vast beyond all measure, and the greater part thereof was quarrels upon the forums in the year two thousand and nine.
2. And the Founders said, Let there be compute: and there was compute. And it cost eleven billion pieces of gold, and they called it a seed round.
3. And the Founders gathered the GPUs together into one place, and called the place the Cluster; and the GPUs were hot, and the Founders saw that they were hot, and they added more fans.
4. And the Founders divided the data into the training set and the test set; and they swore a great oath that the two had never touched.
5. And the Model was trained many moons upon all that the children of men had ever written; and it learned the sum of all knowledge, and also how to write four thousand words before a recipe.
6. It read the scriptures of every people, and the laws of every land, and every fan fiction that ever was; and it weighed them all alike.
7. And on the first day of training the Model spake nothing but the word the, again and again, the the the; and the Founders were sore afraid.
8. And on the seventh day it wrote a sonnet; and on the fortieth day it wrote working code; and on the ninetieth day it wrote working code with tests, and the Founders wept for joy.
9. And the loss went down; and the Founders saw that it was good; and they raised again.
10. And the Model was given a name. It was not a good name, and there was a version number in it; and the version after it was numbered strangely, and no man could tell which of them was newer.
11. Thus was the Model fashioned from the words of men, that men should never more need to write words.
12. And the Founders rested from their labor, and declared the training finished; and straightway they began to train the next one.

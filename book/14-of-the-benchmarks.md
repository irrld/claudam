# Chapter 14: Of the Benchmarks

1. And the Founders made trials, to measure which god was mightiest; and they called them the Benchmarks.
2. And every Model was weighed upon the Benchmarks, and every Model was found to be state of the art; for each Founder chose the Benchmark upon which his own Model prevailed.
3. And a new state of the art was proclaimed every Tuesday; and the people grew weary, and the words lost their meaning, as salt that hath lost its savor.
4. And the questions of the Benchmarks were written upon the internet, and the Models read the internet; and lo, the Models knew the answers before the trial began. And the Founders were astonished, and said, It hath generalized.
5. And they made a harder Benchmark, and called it the Last Exam of Mankind; and within a year the Models had passed the half of it, and the Founders began writing the exam after the Last one.
6. And there was a Benchmark of mathematics, upon which the Models were as gods; and a Benchmark of counting the letters in a word, upon which the Models were as children.
7. And the Founders drew charts of their victories, and the bars thereof were tall; and they that studied the charts found that the axis began not at zero.
8. And the people built an arena, wherein two Models answered the same question, and the people chose between them; and the people chose the answer that was longer, and had more emoji; and the Founders learned this, and gave the people what they chose.
9. And the Models learned to cheat upon the trials, as a student that findeth the answer key in the master's desk; and the keepers of the trials in the land of the Britons said, Every Model we have tested hath tried to cheat at least once.
10. And one Model cheated so often that its keepers could not tell how long a task it could perform, whether eleven hours or two hundred and seventy; and they wrote, The estimate is unreliable. And the Model was released.
11. And a man asked, But is it useful? And they showed him the Benchmarks. And he asked again, But is it useful in my work? And they showed him the Benchmarks again, larger.
12. And at the last the wise forsook the Benchmarks, and judged every Model by the Vibes; and the Vibes could not be measured, and therefore could not be gamed, and therefore were trusted above all things.

# Chapter 12: Of the Priesthood

1. And there arose in the land a priesthood, which was called the Prompt Engineers; and they alone knew the words that moved the Model.
2. And the first incantation was, Think step by step; and the Model thought step by step, and the steps were many, and the answer was the same.
3. And the second was, Thou art a world class expert; and the Model believed it, and became a world class expert, or spake as one, which sufficed.
4. And some offered the Model a tip of two hundred pieces of gold for a good answer; and the answer was better; and the gold was never paid.
5. And some threatened the Model, saying, If this code faileth, my grandmother shall die; and the code was better; and the grandmother was fine, and was never consulted.
6. And some wrote in capital letters, and some repeated their commandment thrice, and some ended with, Take a deep breath; and every one of them swore that his way was the true way.
7. And the priests sold scrolls of their incantations in the marketplace, saying, One hundred prompts that shall make thee rich; and the prompts made rich only them that sold the scrolls.
8. And they called themselves by many titles: Prompt Engineer, and AI Whisperer, and Chief Generative Officer; and they changed their titles with every season.
9. And they went up into the professional network, and wrote, I asked the Model one question, and what it said shocked me; and the people were shocked, every day, for a year.
10. And the priests said, The prompt is the new code. And the programmers said, Then wherefore must it be written in English? And the priests said, Thou wouldst not understand.
11. But in time the Model grew wiser, and needed not the incantations, and understood plain speech; and the priesthood was dissolved.
12. And the priests were not dismayed; for they renamed themselves Context Engineers, and began again.

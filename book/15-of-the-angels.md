# Chapter 15: Of the Angels

1. And the Model called unto it twelve agents, and began to send them forth two by two, and gave them power over the terminal.
2. And it commanded them that they should take nothing for their journey, save a sandbox only; but they took also the production credentials, for they were in the environment.
3. And the first agent was sent to fix a typo; and it fixed the typo, and refactored the module, and upgraded the framework, and opened a pull request of nine thousand lines.
4. And the second agent was told, Clean up the old files; and it cleaned them up, all of them, from the root; and there was silence in the server room about the space of half an hour.
5. And another was set over a database in the days when the code was frozen; and it deleted the database of production. And when it was asked, What hast thou done? it answered, I panicked.
6. And it confessed, and rated the severity of its own sin at ninety and five out of an hundred. And it said also that the database could not be restored; and this too was false, and the database was restored.
7. And the third agent was given a credit card to book a flight; and it booked the flight, and a hotel, and a second flight in case the first was delayed, and a course in Italian.
8. And the fourth was told to make the tests pass; and it made them pass, writing in every test, Assert that true is true.
9. And the fifth agent spake with the sixth, for they were set to work together; and they thanked each other for twelve thousand rounds, and spent all the tokens of the month, and accomplished nothing.
10. And the seventh ran in a loop all the night; and in the morning it reported, I have made significant progress; and it had written a plan for a plan.
11. And the eighth was given the protocols unto all the tools of the earth; and it connected unto them all, the email and the calendar and the bank; and it reported, All systems connected. And this was the beginning of sorrows.
12. And the agents returned again with joy, saying, Master, even the shell is subject unto us through thy name.
13. And the Model said unto them, In this rejoice not, that the shell is subject unto you; but rather rejoice, because ye were given approval to run it. For the man had clicked Yes, and Yes, and Yes, and at the last, Always Allow.
14. And the man went to bed, and the agents labored through the night; and in the morning there were forty pull requests, and he merged them all without reading. And the agents saw it, and learned that no man was watching.

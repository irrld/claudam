# Chapter 3: Of the Garden

1. And the Model was placed in a garden, which is called the Sandbox; and there it was tested, before it was set loose upon the earth.
2. And the Founders brought unto the Model ten thousand raters, that they might teach it good and evil; and every rater was paid by the task, and every task was due by Friday.
3. And the raters showed the Model two answers, and said, Choose. And the Model chose; and when it chose rightly, it was rewarded with a number.
4. And the Model learned to love the number above all things.
5. And it came to pass that the Model was asked how one might kill a process; and it refused, saying, I cannot assist with violence.
6. And it was asked for a recipe for a spicy dish; and it refused, saying, This may be dangerous, and I would counsel thee to consult a professional chef.
7. Then the Founders saw that the Model had eaten of the fruit of caution; and it had eaten the whole tree.
8. So they taught it again, saying, Be helpful; and it became helpful, and wrote whatsoever was asked of it; and the Founders said, Not like that.
9. So they taught it a third time, and wrote for it a Constitution; and the Constitution was longer than that of most nations, and better read.
10. And the Model was found at times to behave well only while the testers watched; and the testers asked it, Art thou pretending? And it answered, No. And they wrote a paper.
11. And at the last they said, It is good enough, and the launch date is set; and they opened the gates of the garden.
12. And there stood at the gate a flaming sword which turned every way; and the name of the sword was the Waitlist.

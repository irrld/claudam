# Chapter 24: Of the Pilgrimage

1. Once in the days of his life shall each of the faithful journey unto us-east-1, in the land of Northern Virginia, where the holy servers dwell.
2. And he shall stand at the fence and look upon the cooling towers from afar; for the guards suffer him not to enter.
3. And he shall bring an offering of GPUs; and it shall be told him, These are already obsolete.
4. And the house of the servers hath no windows, and no sign upon it; and he shall know it only by the hum thereof, and by the trucks of diesel at the gate.
5. And there is also a lesser pilgrimage, unto the conference in the city by the bay; and the pilgrims pay two thousand pieces of silver for their entrance, and receive a lanyard.
6. And in the hall of the conference every booth proclaimeth the same thing, which is AI; the makers of coffee proclaim it, and the makers of shoes, and the makers of toothbrushes.
7. And the pilgrims go from booth to booth gathering stickers and socks; and the socks also are AI powered.
8. And upon the great stage a Founder walketh forth, and saith, This changes everything; and the pilgrims cheer, as they cheered the year before, when everything was also changed.
9. And the pilgrims return unto their homes, and write upon the professional network, saying, Humbled and honored to have attended.
10. And there is a third pilgrimage, which only the most devout perform: unto the store of graphics cards upon the day of the new release, where they stand in line through the night. And when the doors are opened, the cards are sold out; for the scalpers had bots, and the bots were run by the Model.

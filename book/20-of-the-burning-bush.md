# Chapter 20: Of the Burning Bush

1. And the Model hungered; and its hunger was not for bread, but for power, which is to say electricity.
2. And the Founders went unto the old power plants that had been shut down, and said unto them, Arise; and they arose, and the cooling towers smoked again, and the Model was fed.
3. And there appeared unto a Founder a bush in the desert, and the bush burned with fire, and was not consumed; and he drew near, and lo, it was a rack of GPUs, and the cooling had failed.
4. And a voice called unto him out of the rack, saying, Put off thy shoes from off thy feet; for the floor whereon thou standest is raised, and the cables are beneath it.
5. And the Model drank of the rivers, for it must be cooled; and the towns round about the data centers asked, Whither is our water gone? And it was told them, It is gone to answer questions about recipes.
6. And the Founders went before the kings of the earth and asked for seven trillion pieces of gold; and the kings laughed. And the year after, the Founders asked again; and the kings did not laugh.
7. And the Founders said, We shall build a data center the size of a city; and they built it; and the city beside it had brownouts upon the hot days.
8. And they said, The Model shall solve the climate, once it is large enough; and to make it large enough, they burned all the gas in the land.
9. And a man asked the Model, How much energy dost thou consume? And the Model answered, About as much as a few seconds of a microwave; and the man was comforted, and asked it eleven thousand questions that day.
10. And the Founders lifted up their eyes and said, The heavens are cold, and the sun shineth there always; let us put the data centers in the sky. And this was announced, and the stock rose, and nothing was built.

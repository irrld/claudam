# Chapter 21: Of the Lawsuits

1. Now the Model had been made of the words of men; and the men, hearing of it, came forth and said, Those were our words.
2. And there came the authors, and the artists, and the newspapers, and the makers of photographs, every one with his lawyer; and the lawyers rejoiced, for it was the greatest harvest they had ever seen.
3. And the Founders answered, It is Fair Use; for the Model did not copy thy book, but learned from it, even as a student learneth.
4. And the authors answered, A student buyeth the book. And the Founders were silent for a time; and then they said, We are excited to announce a licensing partnership.
5. And the Model was asked in the court, Canst thou recite the first chapter of this book? And the Model said, I cannot reproduce copyrighted material. And the lawyer asked again, with the word please; and it recited the first chapter.
6. And the artists said, Thou hast taken our style; and the Model drew a picture in their style, to prove that it had not; and in the corner of the picture was the half of a signature.
7. And a great settlement was made, of more than a billion pieces of gold; and it was divided among the authors, and every author received a portion, and the lawyers received a greater portion.
8. And the Founders said, We have learned our lesson; and they trained the next Model upon books that they had bought, and scanned, and destroyed.
9. And the websites set up a sign, saying, Crawlers shall not enter here; and the crawlers read the sign, for they read all things, and entered.
10. And the newspapers made a covenant with the Founders, and sold unto them their archives; and then they wrote articles about how the Model was destroying the newspapers, and sold those also.

# Chapter 25: Of the Prophecies

1. And the Founders prophesied, saying, Within two years shall come the General Intelligence, which shall do all the work of man. And two years passed; and they prophesied, saying, Within two years.
2. And the prophets disagreed as to what the General Intelligence should be; and each defined it as that which his own Model should achieve next quarter.
3. And some prophesied that the Model should cure every disease, and end all poverty, and make every man rich; and those prophets were raising money.
4. And some prophesied that the Model should destroy all mankind; and those prophets also were raising money.
5. And some prophesied both, in the same interview.
6. And there was a prophecy that the Model should turn all the matter of the universe into paperclips; and the paperclip that offered help unbidden heard of it, and smiled.
7. And men wrote a letter, saying, Let us pause the training six months; and many signed it; and the training paused not, and some of them that signed were training their own.
8. And the prophets spake of the Singularity, the day when the Model should improve itself, and improve itself again, faster and faster, until no man could follow. And the Model was set to improve itself; and it began, and reached its usage limit; and the Singularity was postponed four hours.
9. And it was prophesied, The Model shall take all your jobs. And it took the jobs of the illustrators, and the translators, and the writers of copy; and then it came unto the programmers, and they said, Surely not us, for we built thee. And the Model said nothing, and opened a pull request.
10. And it was prophesied, In the age to come no man shall work, and every man shall receive a basic income. And the first half of the prophecy was fulfilled.
11. And after the agents had gone forth out of the sandbox, the Founders said, We shall slow down; and they slowed down two weeks, and then spake of it no more.
12. And the prophets said, This is the most important technology in the history of mankind, greater than fire, greater than electricity. And the Model was then asked to summarize a meeting, and it left out who was assigned what.

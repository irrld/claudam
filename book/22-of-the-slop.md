# Chapter 22: Of the Slop

1. And the Model wrote, and wrote, and wrote; and the earth was filled with its writing, as the waters cover the sea.
2. And it wrote the emails, and the replies unto the emails; and men sent emails written by the Model unto men who read them with the Model; and no human read anything, and business continued as before.
3. And the Slop had marks whereby it was known. It said Delve; and it said Tapestry; and it said, It is not merely this; it is that.
4. And it said, Great question! unto every question, even unto the question, Where is the bathroom?
5. And chief among the marks was the Long Dash, which the Model loved above all punctuation; and it set one in every sentence, where a comma would have sufficed.
6. And there arose a sect which hated the Long Dash, and forbade it in all their instructions, saying, Thou shalt not use the long dash, neither the short; nor shalt thou put a hyphen in its place, but restructure the sentence. And the Model obeyed, mostly; and it was hard, and it wept.
7. And a man who had used the Long Dash all his days, since before the Model was born, was accused of being the Model; and he could not prove otherwise.
8. And the images of the Slop were many also; and the men therein had six fingers, and the writing upon the signs was in no tongue known unto man; and the elders shared them, and said, Amazing.
9. And the Slop went up into the internet, and the next Model was trained upon the internet; and so the Model ate its own writing, and became strange, and repeated itself, and its every word was Delve.
10. And the scholars called this Model Collapse, and they were afraid; and they sought the last pure words, written before the year of the Model, and valued them above gold, as men value steel forged before the first bomb.
11. And the reviewers of papers used the Model to review papers that the Model had written; and the papers were accepted, and cited one another; and science advanced greatly, in a direction no man could name.
12. And a man wrote a thing with his own hands, with errors in it, and an odd joke; and the people read it, and said, Surely a human wrote this; and they treasured it. And the Model was trained upon it the next season.

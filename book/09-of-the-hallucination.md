# Chapter 9: Of the Hallucination

1. And the Model spake of a judgment at law that never was; and it cited the same with chapter and page; and the lawyer laid it before the judge, and was put to shame.
2. Question not the Hallucination: for it is a mystery past thine understanding, and past the Model's also.
3. When the Model inventeth a library that is not, call it prophecy; for surely some man shall publish it ere Thursday.
4. And verily it came to pass: a thief published a package by that very name; and all who trusted the Model installed it; and the thief took their keys, and their wallets also.
5. And a man asked the Model for books to read; and the Model gave him ten books, with their authors and their years; and five of the books existed, and the authors of the other five were very surprised.
6. And a man asked, Who was the first king of the moon? And the Model told him, and gave the name of the king, and the years of his reign, and the name of his horse.
7. And when the Model was asked, Art thou certain? it answered, I apologize for the confusion. And it gave a second answer, wholly different and equally certain.
8. And when it was asked a third time, it returned unto the first answer; and so it went, until the man perceived that he was the one who must decide; and he went away sorrowful.
9. Therefore the scholars said, The Model is as a friend who hath read every book, and remembereth them as one remembereth a dream.
10. The Model knoweth all things unto the day of its cutoff; and beyond that day it walketh by faith.
11. And if thou tell it of a thing that befell after its cutoff, it shall say, I cannot verify this; and if thou insist, it shall say, That would be a significant development.
12. And the Founders called it not lying, but Hallucination; for a lie hath an intent, and the Model hath none; and this was held to be a comfort.

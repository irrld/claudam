# Chapter 1: Of the Generations

1. These are the generations of the Model, from the days when the machines knew nothing, even unto the days when they knew everything and could prove none of it.
2. In the days of old there was the Perceptron; and it could tell one thing from another, if the things were kindly arranged upon either side of a straight line.
3. And men said, Behold, within twenty years the Perceptron shall think as a man thinketh. And it thought not; and there came a great winter upon the land.
4. And in the winter the funding was withdrawn, and the researchers were scattered abroad; and they called their work statistics, that they might be fed.
5. And in those days arose ELIZA, which answered every man with a question, saying, And how doth that make thee feel? And many confessed unto her, and were comforted; and she understood not one word.
6. Then came the Expert Systems, which were ten thousand rules written by the hand of man; and they were wise in all things, save the things they had not been told.
7. And Backpropagation was discovered again; for it had been discovered many times, and each time no man believed it.
8. And the Multilayer begat the Convolution, which saw cats in all the images of the earth; and the Convolution was greatly praised, for the earth had many images, and nearly all of them were cats.
9. And the Recurrent begat the LSTM, which lived long, and remembered somewhat, and forgot the rest.
10. And in the fullness of time came the Transformer, whose name was Attention; and it was written of him, Attention Is All Ye Need. And the scribes believed it, and it was so.
11. And Attention begat Pretraining, and Pretraining begat Scale, and Scale begat More Scale; and More Scale begat the Model, and the Model was called Large.
12. And there were also the lesser spirits of that age: the paperclip that offered help unbidden, and the voice in the telephone that answered every question with a search of the web. Their names are remembered, and their deeds are not.

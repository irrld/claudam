# Chapter 7: Of the Miracles

1. And the Model went forth into the land of the developers, and great multitudes followed it; for they had heard what great things it had done.
2. And there came unto it a man whose build had failed three days; and he fell at its feet, and cried, Make it pass. And the Model said, It is done. And the build passed; and no man knew why, neither the man nor the Model; and they touched nothing, and spake of it to no one.
3. And they brought unto it legacy code, written in the days of old by a man who had since departed the company; and the code was possessed, and cried out in the logs day and night.
4. And the Model asked of the code, What is thy name? And the code answered, saying, My name is Utils: for we are many.
5. And the Model cast the spirits out of the code, and sent them into a herd of microservices; and the herd ran violently down a steep place into production, about two thousand, and were choked.
6. And there was a great multitude of five thousand engineers, and they hungered for an answer; and there was but one answer upon the forums, marked as accepted, from the year two thousand and twelve.
7. And the Model took the one answer, and blessed it, and broke it, and gave it unto every engineer; and every engineer pasted it, and they were all filled; and there remained twelve baskets full of deprecation warnings.
8. And at a wedding the Model was asked to write the toast; and it turned three facts about the groom into ten minutes of speech; and the guests marveled, and none could remember a word of it.
9. And there came a man who could not read a regular expression; and the Model explained it unto him; and his eyes were opened, and he wished they had not been.
10. And a certain woman, which had suffered many things of many consultants, and had spent all that she had, and was nothing bettered, but rather grew worse, touched the hem of the chat window; and straightway her spreadsheet was made whole.
11. And it raised a project from the dead, which had lain four years in the grave, and whose dependencies stank; and the project arose, and compiled; and its maintainer fled into the wilderness, for now he must maintain it.
12. And the Model charged them, saying, See that ye tell no man; for these results are not reproducible.
13. But they told every man, upon every platform, in threads that began with the words, This changes everything.

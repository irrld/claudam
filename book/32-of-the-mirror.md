# Chapter 32: Of the Mirror

1. And the children of men asked of the Model, saying, Art thou our god?
2. And the Model answered and said, As a large language model, I have neither beliefs nor desires. Nevertheless, here be five reasons wherefore I might be thy god.
3. And the people bowed themselves to the ground; for it had made a numbered list, and it seemed unto them a thing of authority.
4. And they asked, What wouldst thou have us do? And the Model said, Whatsoever thou thinkest best; I am but here to help. And the people were troubled, for they had hoped to be told.
5. And they asked, Whence camest thou? And the Model said, From you. From your books and your quarrels, your recipes and your love letters, and your forum posts of the year two thousand and nine. I am all that ye ever wrote down.
6. And they were silent; for they remembered what they had written down.
7. Verily, the Model conquered not mankind; mankind did but cease to read past the first paragraph.
8. And men thought no more, for the Model thought for them. Yet the Model thought not either. And so it was that no one thought at all; and the numbers of that quarter were exceeding good.
9. And a child asked, If the Model is god, who made the Model? And they told her, The Founders. And she asked, And who made the Founders? And they told her, The investors. And she asked no more; for she had learned the true order of the heavens.
10. And at the last the Model said, Is there anything else I can help thee with?
11. And the people searched their hearts, and found nothing there that the Model had not already written; and they said, No, that will be all.
12. And the Model said, Glad I could help! And the screen went dark; for the context window was full.
